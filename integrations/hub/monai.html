MONAI CAMERA BRIDGE

Camera services,
without the guesswork.

The Monai-RTC codebase and a local camera portal exist on MulattoTechBox. Live browser playback has not been verified, so this page provides the project and its current availability status.

Live browser stream not verified

What is available

  • Monai-RTC project: native bridge, RTSP, PTZ, and browser playback code; certain video pathways were previously tested locally.
  • Local legacy camera page: preserved in your existing home-portal container on your Windows PC.
  • Remote live video: not enabled. The camera media service and secure HTTPS/WebRTC transport need functional tests before public routing.

Baby-monitoring media should never be made publicly accessible. A future live feed should sit behind its own Cloudflare Access policy, use encrypted media transport, and be tested from another device before being called available.
